## Context
What is the business, technical, or user context? What problem are we solving? What forces are at play (security, browser constraints, performance, privacy)?

## Decision
What is the specific architectural or design decision we are committing to?

## Rationale
Why did we choose this approach over alternatives? What trade-offs were evaluated?

## Consequences
- **Positive**: What benefits or guarantees does this decision provide?
- **Negative / Risks**: What costs, trade-offs, or constraints does this introduce?
- **Compliance & Privacy**: Any implications on store reviews, permissions, or user privacy.
